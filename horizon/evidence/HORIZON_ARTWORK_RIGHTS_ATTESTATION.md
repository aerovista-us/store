## Purpose

This packet documents the chain of rights required to display, advertise,
reproduce, manufacture, distribute, and sell Horizon artwork. Printful will
produce, print, fulfill, and ship physical products. Square will process
payment. AeroVista's commerce backend remains authoritative for customer
pricing, sellability, quotes, and checkout totals.

This is an operational record, not legal advice or a copyright registration.
If ownership is disputed, a contributor is involved, or exclusive rights were
previously granted, obtain legal review before publishing the affected work.

## Entity and attribution policy

- **Legal rights holder and commercial operator:** AeroVista LLC.
- **Horizon:** A division/brand operated under AeroVista LLC, not a separate
  legal rights holder.
- **Public artwork attribution:** No individual photographer/capture-operator

- **Optional public notice:** `© 2026 AeroVista LLC. All rights reserved.`

Product pages, metadata, image captions, alt text, receipts, Printful-facing
customer content, and marketing copy must not expose the capture operator's
personal name unless AeroVista LLC later approves a specific exception.

## Reported capture-to-sale workflow

AeroVista LLC reported this current internal workflow on July 25, 2026:

1. Fly the drone and capture the photograph.
2. Remove the SD card from the drone and insert it into the PC.
3. Occasionally edit the photograph with various software.
4. Upload the finished print file to Printful.
5. Sell the resulting product through the AeroVista storefront, with Square
   processing payment.

This is a strong starting chain of provenance because AeroVista LLC controls
the internal capture, editing, provider upload, and sales workflow. The
internal capture operator has privately identified himself and confirmed that
he personally captured all listed photographs while working under AeroVista
LLC. No third-party contributors or licensed elements and no previous
exclusive licenses or ownership claims were declared.

AeroVista LLC is the selected owner. The private signed instrument will state
that qualifying company-scope photographs are works made for hire and, to the
extent any work does not qualify, assign all copyright rights to AeroVista LLC.
This avoids relying on public attribution or informal company affiliation as
the chain of title.

## Strengthened media workflow

Use this workflow for every new sellable artwork:

1. Copy the SD-card capture into a read-only originals archive before editing.
   Preserve the original filename and EXIF metadata.
2. Create a manifest row with the original filename, capture date/time,
   location or working location, aircraft/camera when available, and SHA-256.
3. Edit only a working copy. Record the final print-master filename, dimensions,
   color profile, meaningful crop/composite/AI/stock inputs, and SHA-256.
4. Create storefront renditions and Printful mockups from the final print
   master; do not treat a mockup as the master.
5. Record the Printful product/template and variant IDs alongside the source
   artwork ID. Record the Square item/variation IDs separately.
6. Back up originals, final masters, manifests, and the private signed rights
   packet in at least two controlled locations.

Routine tonal, crop, sharpening, and color edits do not need a detailed
software-by-software log. Any third-party stock, texture, font, AI-generated
element, or contributor does need to be identified with its applicable rights
or license.

## How to provide confirmation

1. Add the print-ready master for each sellable artwork to the controlled
   media intake location. Product mockups are not source-art masters.
2. Record the master filename, SHA-256 checksum, author, capture date, and
   ownership basis in the register below.
3. Use the selected company-ownership basis: identify any applicable
   work-made-for-hire agreement and include a present written assignment of
   any rights that did not already vest in AeroVista LLC.
4. Complete and sign the attestation. A signed PDF or reputable electronic
   signature record is preferred.
5. Store the signed original in a private business/legal records location.
   Record only its document ID, signer, effective date, and verification
   status in this project.
6. If desired, register the photographs with the U.S. Copyright Office. This
   is recommended protection but is not a prerequisite for creating the
   internal rights record.

Do not select "work made for hire" merely because a photograph was requested
or paid for. Use that basis only when a signed agreement and the applicable
legal requirements support it.
