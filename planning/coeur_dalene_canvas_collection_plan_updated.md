## 1. Original collection purpose

This collection presents original aerial photography of Coeur d’Alene, Idaho, emphasizing recognizable local scenery, autumn color, downtown views, Lake Coeur d’Alene, and the surrounding North Idaho landscape.

Primary audiences include local residents, former residents, visitors, vacation-home owners, real-estate offices, hotels, restaurants, professional offices, and buyers seeking meaningful North Idaho artwork.

The active release includes two 20″ × 40″ panoramic canvas prints and one 24″ × 32″ standard-format canvas.

## 8. Product Development Workflow

### Stage 1 — Image Review

- Confirm subject and location
- Check horizon and lens correction
- Inspect sharpness at 100%
- Review noise, shadows, highlights, and sky detail
- Confirm no visible artifacts

### Stage 2 — Product Definition

- Choose final title and subtitle
- Write short and full descriptions
- Assign keywords and tags
- Select initial size
- Set retail price
- Assign SKU

### Stage 3 — Print Preparation

- Create a print-master file
- Confirm printer color-profile requirements
- Set final crop
- Add mirrored or digitally extended wrap
- Verify safe zones around stretcher edges
- Export at the printer’s required JPEG settings

### Stage 4 — Proofing

- Order one sample
- Inspect color accuracy
- Inspect sharpness and detail
- Inspect wrap alignment
- Inspect stretcher construction
- Photograph the completed canvas

### Stage 5 — Listing

- Add product mockups
- Add real product photographs
- Add title, description, size, and price
- Add production and shipping times
- Add return and damage policies
- Add local pickup where available

### Stage 6 — Launch

- Publish storefront listing
- Add collection page
- Post launch graphics
- Add product to local-business outreach materials
- Record launch date and first sale

## 14. Strategic Recommendation

Keep the initial collection focused and premium.

1. Validate the two current 20″ × 40″ products.
2. Add the 72 MP 2:1 panorama as the third flagship product.
3. Add one strong 4:3 image in a 24″ × 32″ format.
4. Introduce the 16″ × 32″ lower-price option after the flagship products are fully presented.
5. Add signed or limited editions only after real customer interest is established.

This creates variety without flooding the storefront with too many sizes and nearly identical listings.
